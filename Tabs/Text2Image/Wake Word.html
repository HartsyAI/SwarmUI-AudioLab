Voice satellites hold a TCP connection open and stream microphone audio here continuously. This engine detects the wake word, transcribes the command that follows, and identifies the speaker. Detections are published on the AudioLabWakeEvents API, so other extensions can react to them.

Train a new wake word

The phrase is synthesized across the engine's TTS voices and a small classifier is fitted to it. Point negative audio at a folder of real room recordings — with too few negatives the false-accept rate is unusable no matter how good the recall looks.
